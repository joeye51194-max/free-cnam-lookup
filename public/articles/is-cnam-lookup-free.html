CNAM guide

Is CNAM lookup free?

Some websites offer free CNAM lookup to users, but telecom lookup data usually has an upstream cost. A free lookup site may cover that cost with advertising, usage limits, caching, or paid upgrades for heavier use.

Why limits exist

Each lookup can create provider costs and server load. Rate limits help keep the tool available, protect against abuse, and prevent automated scraping from making the service too expensive to run.

What free results include

Free results commonly include caller name, carrier, line type, normalized carrier, and broad location. Some records may be missing if the upstream data source has no result for that number.
