Carrier lookup guide

How carrier lookup works

A carrier lookup checks telecom routing and numbering data to identify the provider or network currently associated with a phone number. It may also return line type, location, and normalized carrier information.

Carrier vs normalized carrier

The carrier field may show the raw carrier name from a telecom data source. Normalized carrier is a cleaned-up version that groups or standardizes carrier names when possible.

Line type and location

Line type can describe whether a number appears to be mobile, landline, VoIP, or toll-free. Location often refers to numbering or routing location, not the caller's exact physical location.
